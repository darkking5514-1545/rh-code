1.Introduction

RH Studio PK ("we", "us", "our") built RH Code (package com.rhstudio.rhcode), a code editor and mini IDE for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using RH Code you agree to this policy.

Short version: your projects and files stay on your device. We do not run user accounts and we do not collect or sell your personal data. Some features you choose to use (online compiler, AI assistant, extensions from links, Git, ads) contact third-party services, described below.

2.Information We Do Not Collect

  • RH Code has no sign-up or login, so we do not collect your name, email address or phone number.
  • We do not upload your projects, source code or files to our own servers. We do not operate servers that store your content.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

3.Data Stored on Your Device

The following stays locally on your device:

  • Projects and files you create, import or open (by default in the app's projects folder, or a folder you choose).
  • Settings such as theme, font size, tab size, auto save, and recent files.
  • Extensions you install and your extension choices (active theme, enabled or disabled state).
  • Ad-free timer earned by watching a rewarded video.
  • AI settings, including an API key if you enter one, saved on your device only.

Uninstalling the app removes its private data. Files in a folder outside the app (for example Documents) remain until you delete them.

5.Third-Party Services

Some features send data to third parties only when you use them:

  • Google AdMob (ads): AdMob may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in apps.
  • Online compiler (Judge0): when you run certain languages (such as Python or C) with the online compiler, the code you run and its input are sent to the Judge0 service to be executed, and the output returns to the app. Do not run code containing secrets or personal data. JavaScript and HTML run locally on your device.
  • AI assistant (Google Gemini API): if you use the AI assistant, your prompt and the project context you choose to include (such as code from open files) are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a reply. Do not include confidential information. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Extensions from a link: installing an extension from a URL downloads that file from the address you enter, and the host of that address can see your request (such as your IP address).
  • Git remotes: if you clone, pull or push, your device connects directly to the Git host you specify (such as GitHub) using any credentials you provide, under that provider's policy.

These services have their own privacy policies and we do not control them.

6.Extensions

Extensions can add themes, snippets, language support and commands. Commands run small scripts in an isolated, restricted environment with no network, file or app data access, and only on the text you select or the current file. You should only install extensions you trust. Extensions installed from files or links come from third parties, and we are not responsible for their content.

7.Children's Privacy

RH Code is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads shown are served by AdMob and are subject to Google's policies. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

8.Data Security

Your data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security (screen lock, encryption). Network requests use HTTPS where the service supports it. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Back up important projects.

9.Data Retention and Your Choices

  • We do not hold your content, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. You can delete projects, extensions and settings inside the app or by clearing the app data or uninstalling.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • You can revoke storage permission in Android system settings.
  • You can avoid third-party processing by not using the online compiler, the AI assistant, or link-based installs.

10.Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or similar laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data. Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, most of this is already in your control on your device. For ad-related data, use the controls above or contact the relevant provider. You can also email us and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

11.International Users

The third-party services above may process data in countries other than your own. By using those features you understand your data may be transferred to and processed in those countries under the providers' policies.

12.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
